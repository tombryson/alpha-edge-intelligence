### The Work-Expansion Mechanism

This is **bounded but multiplicative expansion**, not evidence of an infinite recursive loop. Several individually bounded passes amplify one another:

```text
Research archive and two genuinely independent proposal views
  -> every target, current action, exclusion, portfolio argument and play component becomes a review unit
  -> 26 packets per reviewer, with overlapping evidence and whole-book context
  -> per-packet requests for more archived evidence, followed by additional model lookups
  -> 379 findings + 210 questions + 94 scenario entries
  -> provisional allocation + challenge research
  -> recursive traversal of fact IDs in BOTH baselines, ALL reviews and provisional output
  -> 636 selected IDs; 632 pending source checks; 79 batches
  -> five investment-detail batches
  -> final allocation, possible newly cited fact checks, changed-case refresh
  -> writer, consistency check, optional scoped reconsideration and rewritten commentary
```

The release-361 source-check selector was `linked_facts([ideal, current, provisional, reviews])` in `scripts/portfolio_positioning_memo.py`, followed by supersession/forecast comparison-group expansion. `linked_facts` recursively traverses structured records; it does not ask whether a claim can change the adopted investment case. `fact_review_batches` groups the resulting facts in eights.

| Reviewer records | 457 |

These counts overlap; they must not be added together. The provisional target rows themselves cite 50 distinct IDs. **Checking only those 50 or 87 would also be wrong:** material contrary evidence, a credible alternative or a decisive rejected claim may not have been cited by the provisional allocator. The correct selector is the set of decision-relevant supporting and opposing claims, not either the entire review archive or the allocator's preferred citations.

The pending source-check payloads total approximately 2.36 MB across 79 batches; the largest is approximately 78 KB before the surrounding request schema. Unlike the review stage, these are primarily **too many small mandatory downstream tasks**, not all giant individual requests. Raising batch size alone would recreate the large-call problem. Source-document/observation-version reuse and material-claim selection must come first.

The review packet builder checks a 400,000-character grouping threshold but allows an oversized single unit. It co-locates units by serialized size rather than economic identity. One exposure can recur as an ideal target, current target, current-position action, exclusion and play leg. The unit IDs differ, so the invariant that each unit is assigned once does not prevent repeated analysis of the same investment decision. Source lookups then compensate for incomplete local context, producing more findings and more selected facts downstream.

The original request-payload audit correctly diagnosed repeated evidence and large contracts. Some projections were substantially reduced on an older fixture. **It did not establish that the number of generated requests, evidence overlap across every phase, total bytes, or critical-path work had been reduced on this larger fixture.** The 17.51% lossless representation benchmark is useful but does not resolve this expansion.

### Checkpoint and Provider Failures Are Separate

- Provider records establish explicit upstream 504 failures. No application output-token cap was sent. Larger requests sometimes completed, so context overflow is not demonstrated by these records.
- Provider creation timestamps establish 27-28 minutes of local delay before some early requests reached OpenRouter. That interval cannot be called model generation.
- A later research brief (`gen-1790061798-HeP8EkqjmQ51s9x2DpYL`, DeepInfra) completed about 62 seconds after provider creation at 07:23:18 UTC. The app processed it around 07:32:13, roughly eight minutes after provider completion. This is direct evidence of response-processing delay, not a precise breakdown of disk, CPU and lock time.
- Durable checkpoints are necessary, but serializing the whole growing state for minor progress events is not. Full-state copying, record hashing, JSON conversion and repeated artifact writing sit on the request path. The old first-provider-ID callback also waits for a full save while consuming the response stream.
- The local coalescing fix is partial: `asave` can merge covered saves and cached record digests avoid repeated encodes, but `update_partial` still invokes synchronous `save`; `_snapshot` still copies all partial state; `_write_snapshot` still rebuilds checkpoint/artifact views. Frozen digest-comparison snapshots also consume memory. The 21-save synthetic test does not establish acceptable latency or memory at this 24.44 MB artifact size.

Therefore simply deploying the local funding, retry and coalescing patches is **not** a complete solution. More retries could make the oversized workflow take longer unless admission, per-case work and durable-progress design are corrected too.

### What We Learned Financially

The stopped provisional comparison is better than the original drafts in several ways: it explicitly rejects comparing one-year PCE forecasts directly with a ten-year breakeven, separates bullion from miner equities, recognizes severe near-term oil tightness alongside a conditional future supply recovery, and assesses every named scarcity exposure. Useful critique did reach a provisional synthesis.

It nevertheless exposes unresolved defects:

1. **A corrected explanation can preserve an inadequately justified weight.** The comparison retains 25% TIPS from the independent proposal after rejecting that proposal's mispricing argument. It substitutes positive real carry and a suggested maturity-matched ladder, but does not adequately establish why 25% beats a smaller allocation for this high/5+ year mandate. Positive real carry is relevant, not a complete comparison with nominal bonds or an answer to fiscal-confidence hedge risk. TIPS principal is CPI-linked; a ten-year breakeven is a ten-year average market-implied measure. Neither fact establishes the proposed weight. [TreasuryDirect](https://www.treasurydirect.gov/marketable-securities/tips/), [FRED series definition](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/T10YIE).
2. **Missing research still drifts into adverse investment judgment.** The oil leg lists the absence of upstream margin evidence alongside genuine contrary evidence, assigns 5/10 and chooses a 12% target. A concentration trim can be justified independently of a bearish commodity view, but the exact size must separate that portfolio-risk judgment from unresolved producer returns. A missing observation is not evidence that the scarcity thesis is false.
3. **Conditional forecasts risk becoming settled investment premises.** The 2027 supply rebound and price forecast influence the oil trim without a sufficiently explicit comparison of the assumptions, disruption duration, producer cost curves and failure case. This audit does not claim the forecast is false or that the user's bullish view must win; the architectural requirement is to research and compare both paths before expressing unsupported certainty.
4. **Readily quoted markets can dominate harder research.** Sovereign yields are repeatedly available, whereas upstream margins, fertiliser cash flow, uranium contracts and comparative equity valuation remain incomplete. The resulting preference for a large bond sleeve may partly reflect uneven evidence coverage. This is a plausible bias mechanism, not proof of model intent. Research priority must follow decision importance, not ease of finding a quote.
5. **Play decomposition is still economically redundant.** Two submitted ideas have become 12 components. The five producer themes are useful distinct exposures; a relative-value instruction and multiple overlapping gold/bond mechanisms are not each new tradable plays. Preserve their questions as dimensions of the relevant thesis, not duplicated research/review obligations.
6. **Reference integrity still fails outside the main weights.** The gold/bond play's expression labelled `Avoid long-maturity nominal sovereigns` carries asset ID `global_listed_infrastructure_utilities`. This is a concrete semantic mapping error. Its inclusion in the draft does not show a trade occurred; it shows schema-valid strings can still misidentify an exposure. The comparison also records an unresolved semiconductor ideal-exposure relationship.
7. **Narrative funding and portfolio scope remain conditional.** The main targets total 100%, but this does not validate the smaller-change alternative, every capital mapping, or instrument-level suitability. There are no security positions, base currency or tax settings establishing a specific TIPS ladder or producer basket. Proposals must remain distinct from known holdings and executed trades.
8. **Research status remains misleadingly universal.** All 947 facts are `unresolved`, with no reusable completed source checks. This is not a claim that they are all false. It demonstrates that expensive opinions were generated before decisive source checks became an efficient part of the decision process. A high-level `supported` action label cannot substitute for source support and a valid investment inference.

No final writer ran, so this recovery provides no new evidence that the memo's readability or formatting has improved. Preserve the established reader; do not introduce another UI redesign to hide a missing final document.

### Intended Architecture After Rework

```text
Frozen inputs and cutoff
  -> shared source/observation store, with document locators and versioned checks
  -> isolated ideal and current-aware branches (overlap; no holdings/play leakage)
  -> reveal submitted plays; link them to economic cases rather than duplicate exposures
  -> independent reviewers consume case dossiers and return keyed material findings
  -> resolve a finding using existing evidence first
       -> inspect an existing passage if needed
       -> new focused Parallel research only if it can change a material decision
  -> update affected claims/cases and verify decisive new support AND contrary evidence
  -> compare candidate shapes, common-factor risks, alternatives and funding
  -> settle recommendation with explicit case-level uncertainty
  -> write once; targeted corrections and supporting audit kept separate

Alongside every step: small durable request/result events, versioned outputs,
fair ready-task admission, explicit cancellation and measured remaining work.
```

An event journal can use the existing storage/runtime; this does not require a new agent platform or external workflow engine. Source records and full responses stay archived. No model output ceiling, commodity allowlist, forced fixed asset count, compulsory full rerun, blanket fact-check deletion or increase in provider concurrency is proposed as a shortcut. Materiality governs **which work is necessary**, not which investment opinion is allowed.

## Remaining Financial Deficiencies

### 1. The central bond inference is still wrong in the drafts

Those periods and indices are not comparable. FRED defines its ten-year breakeven as a measure of average expected inflation over the next ten years. Current one-year CPI and a one-year PCE forecast do not establish that the ten-year market price is wrong. The primary series currently also confirms 2.33% for September 18; a second extracted 2.35% should be reconciled by series/date before treating it as disagreement. ## Original Remediation Priorities

1. **Handle temporary in-flight funding separately.** Preserve the structured provider reason and Retry-After. Let accepted calls settle, refresh account state, then retry only the confirmed rejected request. A 402 with no accepted generation must not trigger a duplicate whole analysis. Genuine account exhaustion remains a recoverable stop.
2. **Redesign review packaging around economic cases.** Share a compact whole-book argument, carry only relevant detailed facts per case, and explicitly label omitted cases as assigned elsewhere. Deduplicate common factual corrections and overlapping play questions. Retain full evidence in the archive and targeted lookup capability; do not solve this with output truncation.
3. **Schedule reviewers fairly and control in-flight spending.** Interleave independent reviewer work and react to provider reservations. Cost forecasts should use actual planned packets and roles, not only a historical request count.
4. **Prioritise research by decision materiality.** Use the existing macro evidence; give the largest positions and unresearched submitted plays a fair research budget before repeated macro refinements.
5. **Make evidence absence local and explicit.** Distinguish not retrieved, retrieved but not verified, outside this packet, and genuinely insufficient for the investment inference. Refresh the affected case when evidence changes.
6. **Measure and coalesce checkpoint work.** Preserve accepted outputs durably, avoid whole-state rewrites for every blocked/unchanged status, and record copy/hash/write/lock timings to resolve the 81-minute tail.
7. **Verify outcome quality before calling the uplift complete.** Replay funding and packet-scope counterexamples offline, use the saved real-run workload as a regression fixture, then finish a controlled comparison. The acceptance test is a coherent delivered memo, not merely more passed helper tests or a larger fact ledger.
